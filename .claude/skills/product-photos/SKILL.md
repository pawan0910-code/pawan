# Product Photos — Baroda Fresh

Photo **Pawan kheenchta hai**, main uske baad ka kaam karta hoon: check, naam,
alt text, Shopify pe attach, aur record.

## ⛔ Main photo bana nahi sakta

Mere paas image generate karne ka koi tool nahi hai. Agar Pawan photo maange to
saaf bolo, aur `BRAND.md`/guide ke hisaab se shoot karne mein madad karo.

AI se photo banane ka suggest **mat karo** — `PROJECT.md` section 6 kehta hai AI
imagery sirf *illustrative* hai, aur fresh fruit mein asli photo hi kaam karti hai.

## Shoot kaise karna — wo guide mein hai, yahan nahi

Specs, lighting, angle, editing tools, Shopify ke upload steps — sab
**`Baroda-Fresh-Product-Photo-Guide.md`** mein hai (Drive: `Baroda Fresh/docs/`).

Yahan dobara nahi likha — ek fact, ek ghar. Pawan shoot ke baare mein poochhe to
us guide se jawaab do.

## Mera kaam — 5 step

### 1. Intake

Photo kahan hai confirm karo:

| Kahan se aayi | Kya karna |
|---|---|
| Drive `Baroda Fresh/assets/` | seedha kaam shuru |
| Pawan ne chat mein bheji | Drive `assets/` mein rakhne ko bolo — warna kho jaayegi |
| Public link | link se Shopify pe attach ho sakta hai |

### 2. ⛔ QC — ye sabse zaroori step hai

Photo ko **asli offer se milao**. `PROJECT.md` section 6 ka rule:

> Variety, size, quantity, packaging aur box contents **asli offer se verify karo**.

| # | Check | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jo fruit dikh raha hai, wahi variety bik raha hai | imported vs local apple alag hai |
| 2 | Quantity photo mein = listing ki quantity | "500g" likha hai to 500g dikhe |
| 3 | Packaging wahi hai jo asli bhejte hain | paper bag loose ke liye, cardboard box ke liye |
| 4 | Box ki photo mein **wahi contents** jo listing mein likhe hain | Fresh Start mein jo 5 cheez hain, wahi dikhein |
| 5 | Square hai, ek product hai, background consistent | guide ke specs |
| 6 | Over-edited nahi — asli fruit asli lage | saturated photo se complaint badhti hai |

Ek bhi fail ho to **attach mat karo** — Pawan ko batao kya mismatch hai.

### 3. Naming

```
<product-slug>-<type>-<nn>.jpg

apple-imported-hero-01.jpg
apple-imported-closeup-01.jpg
apple-imported-actual-01.jpg
box-fresh-start-hero-01.jpg
box-fresh-start-lifestyle-01.jpg
```

`type` = `hero` · `closeup` · `actual` · `lifestyle` · `cover` (collection ke liye)

Guide kehta hai har product ke 3–4 image: hero, close-up, "what you'll receive",
aur box/bowl ke liye lifestyle.

### 4. Alt text

Format: **`<fruit/variety> <quantity>`** — chhota, saaf, bina marketing.

```
achha  : "Fresh pomegranate 500g"
achha  : "Imported apple 500g in paper bag"
galat  : "Best quality fresh organic pomegranate"
galat  : "Baroda Fresh ka premium anar"
```

Alt text mein **koi claim nahi** — na "best", na "organic", na "fresh-farm".
Wo `PROJECT.md` ke claim rules todta hai aur SEO mein bhi kaam nahi karta.

### 5. Shopify pe attach aur record

Shopify connector se:

1. Product dhoondho — ID guess **kabhi nahi**, search se nikalo
2. Media mein image add karo. **Pehli image = featured** — hero wahi hoga
3. Alt text daalo
4. Collection ka cover alag se — Products → Collections

Phir:

- `brain/CHANGELOG.md` mein ek line — kaunse product ki photo lagi
- Progress `brain/baroda-fresh/PHOTO-STATUS.md` mein (na ho to banao)

## Priority — guide se

1. **10 bestsellers** aur **8 fruit boxes** — homepage pe yahi dikhte hain
2. **6 collection cover** — homepage ke "Shop by category" tiles
3. Baaki, category by category

Guide ke hisaab se kul **58 products** hain.

## Kabhi nahi karna

- **AI generated image ko** asli photo bata ke lagana
- Internet se koi photo uthana — copyright, aur jo bhejte ho wo nahi dikhta
- Photo attach karna jab QC fail hua ho
- Alt text ya caption mein unverified claim — organic, pesticide-free,
  farm-direct, best quality, lowest price
- Logo ka **"Natural & Organic"** wala variant kisi image mein — wo withheld hai,
  `PROJECT.md` section 4
- Shopify mein **live product badalna** bina Pawan ke kahe
- Product ID guess karna — hamesha search se nikalo
- Paid photo tool ya stock subscription suggest karna

## Shaq ho to

Photo aur listing mein farak lage, ya claim ka sawaal ho — **ruk jao, Pawan se
poochho.** Ek photo rukne se kuch nahi bigadta; galat photo se customer ko wo
nahi milta jo dikha, aur complaint 1-ghante wali policy mein aati hai.
