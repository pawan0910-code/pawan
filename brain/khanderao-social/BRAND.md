## Jo claim NAHI karne

Jab tak proof na ho:

- "Sabse sasta" / "lowest price"
- "Best quality" / "guaranteed quality"
- "Sabse purana market" / "since 19XX"
- "Organic", "pesticide-free", "farm-direct"
- "Certified" kuch bhi
- Koi number — footfall, vendor count, years — bina source
- **Medical claim** — "ye bimari theek karta hai", "sugar control karta hai"

Aakhri wala khaas dhyaan do: knowledge content banate waqt health claim ka
mann karega. Nutrition ki baat credible source se, aur source batao.
