## Kabhi nahi karna

- **Unverified claim** — "sabse sasta", "best quality", "sabse purana", "organic",
  "certified", ya koi number bina source
- **Medical claim** — "ye bimari theek karta hai", "sugar control karta hai"
- Nutrition ya health ki baat bina credible source
- **Vendor ka naam, shop, photo ya price** bina unki permission
- **Customer ka photo ya review** bina permission
- **AI generated image ko** asli market ki photo kehna
- **Spelling badalna** — "Khanderao" hi likhna, har jagah
- **Online order lena ya promote karna**
- Password, API key, account credential kisi file mein likhna
- Post publish karna bina `POSTING-SOP.md` ka approval step
- Paid tool ya subscription recommend karna — free aur open-source hi

sabse kharaab combination hai, kyunki baad mein pata nahi chalta kaunsa kya tha.
