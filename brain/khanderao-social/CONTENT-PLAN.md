## Knowledge content ke niyam — ye sabse zaroori hain

Knowledge pillar hi is page ki taqat hai, par **galat jaankari se bharosa ek post
mein toot jaata hai.**

- **Medical claim nahi** — "ye bimari theek karta hai", "ye khane se sugar control
  hota hai" — aisa kuch nahi
- Nutrition ya health ki baat **credible source** se, aur source batao
- "Organic", "pesticide-free", "farm-direct" — **proof ke bina nahi**
- Quality tips wahi do jo tum khud market mein roz dekhte ho — wo asli value hai,
  aur wahi koi copy nahi kar sakta
- Shaq ho to post mat karo. Ek missed post se kuch nahi bigadta; ek galat post se
  bharosa jaata hai
