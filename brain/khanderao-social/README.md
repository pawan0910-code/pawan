## 🔴 Baroda Fresh se iska rishta

**Ye alag business hai.** Baroda Fresh ka project isse independent hai.

Par ek cheez jaan lo: Baroda Fresh ka `PROJECT.md` **isi market ko apna dispatch
origin** batata hai (section 5). Matlab dono ka address ek hai, business alag hain.

Isliye:

- Is project ka content, brand aur voice **Baroda Fresh se nahi uthana**
- Baroda Fresh ka `BRAND.md` yahan **lagu nahi hota** — iska apna brand doc hai
- Dono ko mila ke "ek hi business" **mat** dikhana, kisi post ya caption mein
- Baroda Fresh ki koi policy (delivery charge, min order, timing) yahan **copy nahi karni**
- **Online order yahan se nahi lena** — wo Baroda Fresh ka kaam hai

Spelling: **Khanderao** sahi hai (Pawan ne 21 Oct 2026 ko confirm kiya). Baroda
Fresh ke `PROJECT.md` section 5 mein "Khandero" likha hai — wo theek hona baaki hai.
