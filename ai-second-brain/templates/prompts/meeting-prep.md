# Prompt: meeting prep from your second brain

From the video ([15:57](https://youtu.be/LqY6hFLMEJw?t=957)). Before interviewing someone from OpenAI, Vaibhav asked his second brain in the car,
and it produced a prep report from months of his own thoughts. "No team can beat this. This is you."

```
Tomorrow I'm meeting [NAME, ROLE, COMPANY] about [PURPOSE].

Search my second brain (Cognee / cards / meetings / chat summaries / digests) and give me:
1. Everything I've said, asked or doubted about [their company / their topic] in the last [3] months, with dates and sources.
2. Debates I've had where my view differed from others'. Questions they could settle.
3. Recent news about them or their company (last 30 days).
4. 5 sharp questions to ask, ranked, each with why it matters to MY goals.
5. 2 things I could offer or ask for.
Keep it to one page.
```

Variants:
- **Podcast or content prep:** "What strong perspectives have I expressed in the last 3 months on [topic] that I haven't published yet?"
- **Decision prep:** "I'm deciding [X]. What have I said about this before, and what evidence do I have for each option?"
